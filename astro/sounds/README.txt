SOUND SLOTS for the Orion Astrolabe home page (for Pep)

Sound is OFF by default. A visitor turns it on with the small "Sound off" button in the bottom-left corner.
Until a file is dropped here, the page plays tiny tones it makes itself in the browser (no downloads).

Drop your own sounds into this folder with EXACTLY these names (mp3), and they are used instead:

  ring-lock.mp3            a small mechanical click when a part of the instrument locks into place
  constellation-chime.mp3  a soft chime when a constellation finishes lighting up, star by star
  supernova.mp3            Betelgeuse flaring (click Betelgeuse 3 times quickly)
  dive.mp3                 the camera diving into a game's ring before its page opens

Keep them short (under 2 seconds, except supernova, up to about 4) and quiet; the page plays them at 70% volume.
Only use sounds we made or own outright. Nothing is downloaded from anywhere by the page itself.
