Small studio,
stubborn about craft.

Orion Lab Studios is two people building the games we wanted to play, by hand, one level at a time.

I

Atmosphere first

Light, music and weather do as much storytelling as words. If a place doesn't feel alive, it isn't finished.

II

Made for every phone

We tune for older and cheaper phones, not just the newest ones, so everyone gets the same game.

III

Fair to players

Clear prices, honest rewards and no tricks. A game should earn your time, not trap it.

Our promise to players and parents

Play should be safe. Here is what we hold ourselves to.

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to anyone.
  • No personalized ads. The ads in our games don't build a profile of you.
  • Kid-safe by design. Our games follow children's privacy rules (COPPA in the U.S., GDPR-K in Europe).
  • Clear prices. Every purchase says exactly what you get before you pay.
  • Your data, your call. Every game has a page to delete your data, and we answer within 30 days.
  • Plain-language policies. Each game has its own privacy policy and terms, linked below.
