The game

Shaft Bound starts with a pickaxe and a broken old mine, and grows into a huge underground operation full of drills, carts, trains and defenses. Every tunnel you dig can become a path for the creatures below, so where you dig decides what you have to defend. The deeper you go, the stranger it gets, and nobody knows what waits at the bottom.

⛏ You build the battlefieldEvery tunnel you dig is a route the monsters can use. Dig smart, then defend it.
⚙ Automation you can watchDrills, carts, conveyor belts, trains and drones run your mine by themselves.
👑 10 bosses, then foreverTen story bosses on the way to 4,000 m, then an endless mine that keeps getting deeper and harder.
⬆ Upgrades to level 100Side missions, collections and a mystery to uncover.
📴 Plays offlineNo sign-in and no internet needed to play. In 18 languages.
✋ One hand, uprightTap rock to dig, tap monsters to bonk them, drag and pinch to look around.

Made for kids and grown-ups

Shaft Bound is for kids and adults. The creatures are cartoons that puff into dust when beaten: no blood, no swearing, and no chat with other players.

  • No accounts and no sign-in. Progress is saved only on your device.
  • No tracking by us. The game sends us nothing.
  • Kid-safe ads. Ads from Google AdMob are child-directed and non-personalised for every player, and never use the advertising ID. No banners, ever.
  • One thing to buy, behind a grown-up question: Remove Ads (one-time).
