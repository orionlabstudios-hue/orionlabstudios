Questions

Is Spell Shore out yet?

Not yet. It's in development. This page will list where to get it once it's released.

What age is it for?

It's designed so a 6-year-old can play it, with gentle spelling help built in.

Are there ads or purchases?

Spell Shore will show ads. The first test versions may have none. Any ads will be child-safe and non-personalised, and our privacy policy will name the ad provider before they appear. There is nothing to buy today, and there will never be loot boxes.

Where is progress saved?

Only on your child's device. We don't keep a copy, so if the app is deleted (or the browser's site data is cleared) progress can't be recovered.

How do I reach the parents' page?

Tap For Parents in the game and answer the grown-up question. See For parents.

How do I delete my child's data?

On the parents' page tap Start the game over, or delete the app. Nothing is stored with us. See Delete data.
