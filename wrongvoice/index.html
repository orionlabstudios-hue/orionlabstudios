The game

Survive the valley. Find Anya.

Wrong Voice is a survival horror game for phones. Survive a plane crash in a remote mountain valley, build a camp, and follow Anya's trail, while something in the valley learns to copy the voices of the people you love.

In development

Wrong Voice is not finished yet.

It is coming to iPhone and Android, for ages 13 and up. Questions? Email support@orionlabstudio.com.
