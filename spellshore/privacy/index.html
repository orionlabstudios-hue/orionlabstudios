Spell Shore

Privacy policy

Sep 30, 2026 · Orion Lab Studios

The short version

  • We collect no personal information from your child or from you. The game never asks for a name, email, age, photo or voice.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking. Spell Shore will show child-safe, non-personalised ads; the first test versions may have none.
  • Progress and learning stats are saved only on your child's device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • You can erase everything at any time from the parents' page.

This policy covers the Spell Shore game made by Orion Lab Studios, in every version we release (web, iPhone, iPad and Android), and this website's Spell Shore pages.

Who we are

Spell Shore is made by Orion Lab Studios ("we", "us"). Contact us about privacy at orionlabstudios@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

What the game stores, and where

WhatWhere it is keptWhySent to us?
Game progress: your island, buildings, fish, settingsOn the device only (in the app's storage, or the browser's local storage for the web version)So your child can keep playingNo

The current version has no accounts or logins, no cloud saves, no analytics or crash reporting, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat, no multiplayer, no leaderboards, no links to other websites or social media, and no notifications. It does not use the microphone, camera or location.

Getting the game

If you download Spell Shore from Apple's App Store or Google Play, the store handles the download under its own privacy policy. We do not receive information about you from the store beyond anonymous totals the stores show every developer.

If you play the web version, your browser downloads the game's files like any web page. The servers that deliver those files, including cdnjs (run by Cloudflare), which delivers a free 3D graphics library the game uses, receive your device's IP address, as every website does. We do not receive or use it. The game's font is built into the game and is not loaded from Google.

Children's privacy

Spell Shore is made for children. We follow children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

  • We do not collect, use or share personal information from children. Because nothing is collected, no parental consent is needed to play.
  • There are no third-party services in the game that collect data: no advertising networks, no analytics and no social features.
  • The parents' page is behind a grown-up question so children don't wander into it.
  • Privacy is on by default. There are no settings a child can change that would share anything.

Ads

Spell Shore will include advertising. The first test versions may not show any. Before any ad appears, we will update this policy to name the ad provider and exactly what it receives, and tell you in the game. Any ads in Spell Shore will be:

  • contextual and non-personalised: never based on tracking your child, building a profile, or following them across apps or websites;
  • served only through ad providers that are certified for children's apps (for example, Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs) and set to their child-directed mode, without sending the device's advertising ID or location;
  • suitable for young children, clearly marked as ads, and behind a grown-up question before any ad can take your child out of the game.

The same applies to any other feature that would send data: we update this policy first, tell you in the game before the change applies, and ask for verifiable parental consent wherever the law requires it.

Who we share data with

Nobody. The current version has no personal data to share, and we never sell data. If we ever use an outside service (such as a child-safe ad provider), we will name it in this policy first, and it must protect data at least as well as this policy does.

How long data is kept

Game progress and learning stats stay on the device until you tap Start the game over on the parents' page, delete the app, or clear the browser's site data for the web version. We keep nothing on our side.

Your choices and rights

  • See your child's data: open the parents' page in the game.
  • Delete it: tap Start the game over, or delete the app. See Delete data.
  • Consent: the current version needs no consent because it collects nothing. If a future version ever needs a parent's permission, you will be able to withdraw it at any time, and we will say how here.
  • Questions or requests: email orionlabstudios@gmail.com. Parents and players in the EU, UK and Switzerland, California and other places with privacy laws have rights to access, correct and delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, a request to us will usually confirm that we hold nothing.

Security

Data stays on the device, protected by the device's own security. Anyone who can use the device can open the game and the parents' page, so please keep your device's lock screen on.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we tell you in the game before they apply.
