Delete data

Shaft Bound by Orion Lab Studios

Shaft Bound keeps your progress only on your device. It has no accounts, and nothing is sent to us. You can erase everything at any time.

How to delete

In the game

Open Settings, tap Reset progress, and confirm.

On your device

Delete Shaft Bound from your device. This removes everything the game saved.

What gets deleted

Your game progress, your settings, the open count and daily-discovery date, and the record that Remove Ads was bought on this device. Your Remove Ads purchase itself stays with your Google or Apple account, so you can get it back with Restore Purchases.

What we hold

Nothing. We have no accounts, names or server data to delete. Information sent to Google AdMob when an ad is requested isn't kept by us; it follows Google's privacy policy.

Questions? Email orionlabstudios@gmail.com (we answer within 30 days), or read our privacy policy.
