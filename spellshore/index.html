The game

A real island adventure that happens to teach spelling.

Spell Shore is a cozy 3D island adventure where kids catch fish by spelling the picture they see, then grow their island, open shops, decorate, and explore a map of new islands. Every night pirate ships sail in, and kids tap to throw coconuts while their walls, cannons and crab army defend the shore. Spelling help is gentle and built into the fishing, so kids learn words while they play.

🎣

Spell to fish

See a picture, spell the word, reel in the catch. Words grow with your child.

🏝

Grow your island

Build homes and shops, decorate, and sail to new islands on the map.

⚓

Pirate nights

Tap to throw coconuts while walls, cannons and a crab army defend the shore.

🦜

Polly helps

Stuck on a word? The right letter wiggles, wrong ones fade, and Polly the parrot lends a wing.

🎁

No pressure

No timers, no streaks to lose. The daily Tide Chest waits for you, up to three days.

👪

A page for grown-ups

A parents' page, behind a grown-up question, shows the words practised and the letters to work on.
